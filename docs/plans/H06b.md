# H06b: combiner and format coverage

Source: one tour of 37 maps on the build of 2026-10-19 (log 3ds/logs/h06b_tour_log.txt, row H06b, 1 Azahar start). The map list is the 9 maps flagged by the older C2R tour (421 maps, build of 2026-10-18, logs 3ds/logs/c2r_tour_log_*.txt) and 28 other maps from all areas.
The audit is comb_audit in 3ds/src/pm_gbi.c. A combo is one key of: combiner words, cycle type, other-mode low word (blender, Z, alpha), texture format and size, legacy mode. It logs "comb new" for each combo that uses a plan or is inexact. A combo is INEXACT when the error of the path used is over 0.012. The other combos use the legacy path and are exact.
Not audited: copy and fill cycles, the dual tile, box and pattern special paths (counted, not judged).

Result: 63 combos logged, 58 exact through the plan, 5 flagged.
