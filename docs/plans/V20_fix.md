## V20a aura around the spirits looks incomplete
- The aura draw (src/effects/aura.c, DL D_09002000_379F80) is two cycle, tile 0 = I8 32x32 clamp, tile 1 = I4 32x64 (LoadMultiTile, wrap in s, mirror in t, mask 5 and 6). The tile 1 window is moved every frame with gDPSetTileSize(1, v1, v2, v1 + 124, v2 + 508): 31 texels in s and 127 texels in t over a 64 texel tile that mirrors.
- Combiner of the aura: c1 (T0-T0)*PRIM+T1 / (T0A-0)*ENVA+T0A, c2 (T0-CMB)*CMB+ENV / (CMBA-0)*PRIMA+0. The log line `comb new f2573 INEXACT ... planerr 764` showed the plan was far from exact. I made it exact (planerr 7, pm_comb.c: COMBINED in cycle 2 takes the texel that cycle 1 copies) and the shots f2632 to f2656 did not change (0.0 percent of the pixels over the tolerance). So the combiner is not what shows. I reverted that change (no benefit visible, risk for other effects).
- Open lead, in a main-owned file: the tile 1 window spans 127 texels in t over a mirrored 64 texel tile. pm_gbi.c t1_fold keeps the draw start inside the window but says "the wrap inside one draw is not reproduced, the part past the window edge reads the neighbour pixels of the slot". The aura quad needs a wrap and a mirror inside one draw. This is the most likely reason for "part of the effect is missing". Test: the aura frames f2632 to f2656 with a debug view of tile 1 only (ddonly on the aura chunks).

## V21 star icon not centered on the cards
- Effect src/effects/something_rotating.c, DL D_09004458 (card), icons D_09003F98 and the six after it. The icon is tile 1: LoadMultiTile_4b 48x48 CI4 (uls 0, lrs 47) then `gsDPSetTileSize(1, 0x20, 0x20, 0xDC, 0xDC)` = window 8 to 55. The card quad has uv 1 to 63 (D_090043D8). The icon is centred on the card on the N64 only when the tile 1 origin offset of 8 texels is applied as "s - 8" to the image that was loaded at 0.
- In the shot (cards_zoom_before.png) the star and its dark oval sit off the card centre. Lead: pm_gbi.c dual_bind: `t1_p[2] = (tile_x0 * rs - (td[1].x0 - ox1)) / tw1`. td[1].x0 is 8 after SetTileSize, ox1 is the origin of the loaded window, tw1 the size of the padded texture (48 padded to 64 by pm_tex.c). If tw1 is 64 but the offset is in 48 texel units, or if SetTileSize moves the image window instead of only the coordinate offset, the icon shifts. Not measured. Next step: log td[1].x0, ox1, tw1, t1_p for these draws (drawlog with the img of D_09001080).
